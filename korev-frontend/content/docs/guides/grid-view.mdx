---
title: Grid view
description: 'Watch chats and terminals from different workspaces side by side.'
---

The grid view shows chats and terminals from different workspaces at the same time. Use it when several agents work in parallel and you want to see all of them.

## Open the grid

Click **Grid** in the sidebar, or press ⌘G.

To pick a layout, click the layout button at the top right. The layouts are 2×1 (two panes side by side), 1×2 (two panes on top of each other) and 2×2 (four panes).

## Put a chat or a terminal in a pane

- Drag a workspace from the sidebar onto a pane.
- Or click **Choose workspace** in an empty pane.

The pane shows the chat or terminal tab you used last in that workspace. To show a different chat or terminal of the same workspace, click its name in the pane header. In that menu, **New chat**, **New terminal**, **Claude Code in a terminal** and **Codex in a terminal** open a new tab in the pane.

A pane terminal is the same terminal as the tab in the full view. It keeps running when you switch between the grid and the full view.

A chat or a terminal shows in one pane at most. If you put it in a second pane, it leaves the first one.

## Work in a pane

Click a pane to focus it. The focused pane has a blue border. The keyboard shortcuts work on the focused pane:

- ⌘L focuses its composer.
- ⌘T starts a new chat in it.
- ⌘W removes it from the grid. The chat or terminal stays open in its workspace.
- ⌘⇧P, ⌘⇧X and ⌘⇧⌫ work on its workspace and chat.

## Go to the full workspace

Click **Open in full view** in the pane header. The workspace opens on that chat or terminal, with its tabs, the Changes panel and the terminal panel. Press ⌘G to go back to the grid.

When you click a file or a diff in a pane, the workspace also opens in the full view.

A click on a workspace in the sidebar always opens the full view. Only a drag puts it in a pane.
